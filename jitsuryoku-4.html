長文読解（英文スピーチ）

▼ 次の英文を読み，あとの各問い（問１〜問４）に答えなさい
次の文章は中学生のミア（Mia）が，「エネルギー」について英語の授業で書いたスピーチです。英文を読んで，あとの各問いに答えなさい。

My name is Mia. I am a student at Sakura Junior High School. Last year, my school installed *solar panels on the roof. This made me very interested in solar energy.

Before that, I didn't know much about energy. One day, our science teacher, Ms. Kimura, explained how solar panels work. She said, "Solar panels can turn sunlight into *electricity. （ １ ） They are good for the environment because they don't produce any *harmful gases." I was surprised to learn this.

After the lesson, I wanted to learn more, so I did some research on the Internet. I found out that Japan gets a lot of sunlight, so solar energy can be very useful here. However, I also learned that solar panels are still expensive to install. （ ２ ） I began to wonder how we can make solar energy available for more people.

I decided to write a report about solar energy. I interviewed Ms. Kimura, and she told me more about the future of energy. She said, "Many countries are now trying to use more *renewable energy. （ ３ ） Wind power and water power are good examples. These energy sources will become more and more important in the future."

When I finished my report, I *presented it to my class. My classmates were very interested. Some of them said they wanted to try using solar energy at home. （ ４ ） I was happy that my report could inspire them to think about the environment.

Now I am more interested in energy and the environment. I think it is important for young people to think about these problems. If we all make small changes in our daily lives, we can help protect our planet for the future.

（注）*solar panels ソーラーパネル　*electricity 電気　*harmful 有害な　*renewable 再生可能な　*presented 発表した
設問
問１　内容一致（3問）　本文の内容と合うように空所に入る最も適切なものを①〜④から選びなさい
(1)　What did Ms. Kimura tell Mia about solar panels?
(2)　Why did Mia write a report about solar energy?
(3)　After Mia's presentation, her classmates ( ).
問２　文挿入　次の英文を（１）〜（４）のどこに入れるのが最も適切か，番号で選びなさい
"But now the prices are slowly becoming lower, so more people may be able to use them in the future."
問３　日本語で答えなさい（15字程度）
本文中の下線部「these problems」とは，どのような問題のことですか。本文の内容をふまえて，日本語で15字程度で書きなさい。
模範解答：エネルギーと環境に関する問題。（13字）
「エネルギー」「環境」のどちらかだけでも可。本文最終段落の内容から読み取りましょう。
問４　空所A〜Cに入る最も適切な英語を1語ずつ書きなさい
Mia goes to Sakura Junior High School. Last year, her school started using ( A ) panels on the roof. She learned about them from her science teacher and became very interested. She wrote a ( B ) about solar energy and presented it to her class. After that, her classmates wanted to think more about the ( C ).
答え：
（A） solar　→　solar panels（ソーラーパネル）
（B） report　→　レポートを書いた
（C） environment　→　環境について考えるよう促された
