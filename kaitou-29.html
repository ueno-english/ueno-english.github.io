ポイント問題・確認問題（教科書 p.146-147）
ポイント問題　次の＿に、whichかthatのうち適する方（どちらでもよい場合はwhich）を書く。また、できた文を日本語に直す
  1. (1) This is the bag ___ I bought yesterday.
  2. (2) She is a girl ___ I sometimes see on the bus.
  3. (3) The cup ___ my brother broke was mine.
  1. which　これはわたしがきのう買ったかばんです。💡 先行詞が物でbought の目的語がない。目的格のwhich。
  2. that　彼女はわたしがバスの中でときどき見かける女の子です。💡 先行詞が人（a girl）なので that。（目的格の whom は発展研究で学ぶ）
  3. which　わたしの兄［弟］が割ったカップはわたしのでした。💡 目的格の関係代名詞は省略できる。
1　●目的格の関係代名詞 which●　例にならって、あとの2つの文をwhichを使って1つの文に書きかえる（例：I like the camera. My uncle gave it to me. → I like the camera which my uncle gave to me.）
  1. I like the picture. Yumi painted it for me.
  2. I'm reading a book. I borrowed it from the library.
  3. These are roses. My grandmother takes care of them.
  4. The watch was really beautiful. Jane's grandmother gave it to Jane.
  5. The fish were all small. My brother caught them in the river.
  1. I like the picture which Yumi painted for me.💡 2文目の目的語 it が消えて、which が文頭に出る。
  2. I'm reading a book which I borrowed from the library.
  3. These are roses which my grandmother takes care of.💡 take care of 〜 の of が最後に残る。
  4. The watch which Jane's grandmother gave to Jane was really beautiful.💡 主語を修飾するので文の中に入る。
  5. The fish which my brother caught in the river were all small.💡 The fish が複数扱いなので were。
2　●目的格の関係代名詞 that●　例にならって、あとの2つの文をthatを使って1つの文に書きかえる（例：Mary is a girl. Mike introduced her to me. → Mary is a girl that Mike introduced to me.）
  1. Mike is the boy. I teach him Japanese.
  2. These are students. Mr. Sato taught them last year.
  3. The child was Mr. Brown's son. I helped him yesterday.
  4. The girls are my sisters. Mr. Brown is talking to them.
  5. Ken's mother will buy him a DVD. He wants it.
  1. Mike is the boy that I teach Japanese.💡 him が消える。teach 人 物 の「人」が先行詞。
  2. These are students that Mr. Sato taught last year.
  3. The child that I helped yesterday was Mr. Brown's son.
  4. The girls that Mr. Brown is talking to are my sisters.💡 talk to 〜 の to が最後に残る。
  5. Ken's mother will buy him a DVD that he wants.
3　●関係代名詞の省略●　次の文には関係代名詞が省略されている。その位置を記号で答える
  1. This is ア a letter イ I ウ received エ yesterday.
  2. The woman ア I イ asked ウ the way エ was オ very kind.
  3. I like ア these pictures イ my mother ウ took エ in Hawaii.
  4. I'll tell ア you イ everything ウ I エ know.
  5. All ア you イ have to do now ウ is エ to call her.
  1. イ💡 a letter [that] I received yesterday。目的格の関係代名詞は《名詞＋主語》の間に入る。
  2. ア💡 The woman [that] I asked the way。
  3. イ💡 these pictures [which] my mother took。
  4. ウ💡 everything [that] I know。
  5. ア💡 All [that] you have to do now。
練成問題（教科書 p.148-149）
1　例にならって、あとの2つの文をwhichかthat（どちらでもよい場合はwhich）を使って1つの文に書きかえる（例：I live in a house. My grandfather built it. → I live in a house which my grandfather built.）
  1. I know a boy. Ann likes him.
  2. This is the sweater. I bought it last Sunday.
  3. The baseball game was really exciting. We watched it on TV.
  4. The woman was very kind. I asked her the way to the station.
  1. I know a boy that Ann likes.💡 先行詞が人なのでthat。
  2. This is the sweater which I bought last Sunday.
  3. The baseball game which we watched on TV was really exciting.
  4. The woman that I asked the way to the station was very kind.
2　次の日本文に合う英文になるように＿に適する語を書く
  1. これは彼が好きな歌です。 This is a song ___ he ___.
  2. わたしが訪れたい都市はローマです。 The city ___ I want to ___ is Rome.
  3. わたしはきのう、あなたがよく知っている女性に会いました。 Yesterday I saw a woman ___ you ___ well.
  4. わたしはシェークスピアが書いた本を読んでいます。 I'm reading a ___ ___ Shakespeare ___.
  5. メアリーがパーティーに連れてきた男の子は彼女の弟でした。 The ___ ___ Mary ___ to the party was her brother.
  1. which [that], likes💡 主語heに合わせてlikes。
  2. which [that], visit
  3. that, know💡 先行詞が人なのでthat。
  4. book which [that], wrote
  5. boy that, brought💡 bring-brought-brought。
3　次の各組の文がほぼ同じ内容になるように＿に適する語を書く
  1. (1) a This is a CD. My brother got it yesterday. b This is a CD ___ ___ ___ ___ yesterday.
  2. (2) a The woman wasn't at home. I wanted to see her. b The woman ___ ___ ___ to see wasn't at home.
  3. (3) a Mary bought a nice racket last week. b The racket ___ ___ ___ last week was nice.
  4. (4) a Who is your favorite baseball player? b Who is the baseball player ___ ___ ___ the best?
  5. (5) a This is a picture taken by my father last Sunday. b This is a picture ___ ___ ___ ___ last Sunday.
  6. (6) a I have never seen such a beautiful flower. b This is the ___ beautiful flower ___ ___ ___ ever seen.
  1. which [that] my brother got
  2. that I wanted💡 先行詞が人。
  3. which [that] Mary bought
  4. that you like💡 「最も好きな選手」＝「あなたが最も好きな選手」。
  5. which [that] my father took💡 taken by my father ＝ my father took。
  6. most, that [which] I have💡 最上級（most）があるときは that が好まれる。
4　次の日本文に合う英文になるように、（　）内の語句を並べかえる
  1. これらはわたしたちが作った料理です。（are / cooked / which / these / the dishes / we）
  2. あなたが道を教えた男性はわたしのおじでした。（was / that / told / you / my uncle / the man / the way）
  3. 弟は借りたビデオを見ているところです。（a video / he / is / my brother / rented / watching / which）
  4. わたしはきのう買ったCDをもう聞いてしまいました。 I have（I / to / bought / listened / that / yesterday / already / the CD）.
  5. 彼女がわたしたちにたずねた質問はとても難しかった。（asked / difficult / she / the questions / us / very / were）
  6. 何かわたしにできることはありますか。（anything / do / I / is / that / can / there）?
  7. あなたが貸してくれた本はとてもおもしろかった。（you / which / was / very / to me / the book / lent / interesting）
  8. 彼がきのう連れてきた少女は英語をとてもじょうずに話しました。（yesterday / well / he / English / very / the girl / brought / spoke / that）
  1. These are the dishes which we cooked.
  2. The man that you told the way was my uncle.💡 told the way「道を教えた」。
  3. My brother is watching a video which he rented.
  4. I have already listened to the CD that I bought yesterday.💡 listen to 〜。不足語 to。
  5. The questions she asked us were very difficult.💡 関係代名詞を使わず省略した形。
  6. Is there anything that I can do?💡 anything のあとは that が好まれる。
  7. The book which you lent to me was very interesting.
  8. The girl that he brought yesterday spoke English very well.
5　次の日本文を、thatを使って英語に直す
  1. これは父が作ったいすです。
  2. 彼女はわたしがきのうここで会った女の子です。
  3. わたしたちの知らないことがたくさんあります。
  4. わたしは彼が1週間前に書いた手紙を読んでいるところです。
  5. これはわたしが公園へ行くために使った自転車です。
  6. わたしがきのうつった魚は父によって料理されました。
  1. This is a [the] chair that my father made.
  2. She is a [the] girl that I saw [met] here yesterday.
  3. There are many [a lot of] things that we do not [don't] know.💡 things を先行詞に。
  4. I am [I'm] reading a [the] letter that he wrote a week ago.
  5. This is a [the] bike [bicycle] that I used to go to the park.💡 「〜するために使った」＝ used to go（目的の不定詞）。
  6. A [The] fish that I caught yesterday was cooked by my father.
