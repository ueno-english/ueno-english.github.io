確認問題・練成問題の解答＋1問ごとの解説
確認問題 練成問題
確認問題（教科書 p.73）
1　●現在完了の継続用法の意味●　次の各組の英文を、下線部に注意して日本語に直す
  1. ① I use this computer every day. ② I have used this computer for three years.
  2. ① My sister was in the kitchen this morning. ② My sister has been in the kitchen since this morning.
  1. ① わたしは毎日このコンピュータを使います。② わたしは3年間ずっとこのコンピュータを使っています。
  2. ① わたしの姉［妹］はけさ、台所にいました。② わたしの姉［妹］はけさからずっと台所にいます。
2　●現在完了の形●　次の文を、（　）内の語句を加えて現在完了の文に書きかえるとき＿に適する語を書く
  1. I play the piano.（since 1997） → I ___ the piano since 1997.
  2. We study English.（for three years） → We ___ English for three years.
  3. Jane lives in Japan.（for five years） → Jane ___ in Japan for five years.
  4. My brother is sick in bed.（since yesterday） → My brother ___ sick in bed since yesterday.
  1. have played
  2. have studied
  3. has lived
  4. has been💡 be動詞の過去分詞はbeen。isの現在完了はhas been。
3　●否定文●　次の文を否定文に書きかえる
  1. I have used this racket for three months.
  2. We have heard from him since he left Japan.
  3. It has rained for a week.
  1. I have not [haven't] used this racket for three months.
  2. We have not [haven't] heard from him since he left Japan.
  3. It has not [hasn't] rained for a week.
4　●疑問文●　次の文を、〔　〕内の指示に従って書きかえる
  1. They have wanted a cell phone for a long time.〔疑問文に書きかえ、yesで答える〕
  2. Ms. Sato has been here since nine o'clock.〔疑問文に書きかえ、noで答える〕
  3. He has known her for five years.〔下線部が答えの中心となる疑問文に〕
  1. Have they wanted a cell phone for a long time? — Yes, they have.
  2. Has Ms. Sato been here since nine o'clock? — No, she has not [hasn't].
  3. How long has he known her?💡 期間をたずねる疑問文はHow long have[has]+主語+過去分詞〜?で表す。
練成問題（教科書 p.74-75）
1　＿に、あとの（　）内の語を必要に応じて文中での適する形に直して書く
  1. I ___ in Tokyo now.（live）
  2. I ___ Australia five years ago.（visit）
  3. I have ___ in Tokyo for five years.（live）
  4. He is ___ the guitar now.（play）
  5. He was ___ in the park then.（run）
  6. This book was ___ by my grandfather.（write）
  7. My sister has ___ a new racket since last month.（want）
  1. live
  2. visited
  3. lived
  4. playing💡 He is playing〜（現在進行形）。isがすでにあるので原形+ing。
  5. running💡 was ___ing（過去進行形）。runは語尾の子音を重ねてrunning。
  6. written💡 write-wrote-writtenの不規則変化。受動態was writtenの形。
  7. wanted💡 has ___（現在完了）。wantは規則動詞なのでwanted。
2　日本文に合う英文になるように＿に適する語を書く
  1. 彼は今、北海道にいます。 He ___ in Hokkaido now.
  2. 彼は先週からずっと北海道にいます。 He ___ in Hokkaido since last week.
  3. 彼らはきのう、熱心に働きました。 They ___ hard yesterday.
  4. 彼らは学校を卒業してからずっと熱心に働いています。 They ___ hard since they left school.
  5. きのうはあまり暑くありませんでした。 It ___ so hot yesterday.
  6. 先月からあまり暑くありません。 It ___ so hot since last month.
  7. あなたはこの前の土曜日、ジュディに会いましたか。— いいえ、会いませんでした。 ___ you ___ Judy last Saturday? — No, ___.
  8. あなたがたは長い間、ずっと知り合いなのですか。— はい、そうです。 ___ you ___ each other for a long time? — Yes, ___.
  1. is [stays]
  2. has been [stayed]
  3. worked
  4. have worked
  5. wasn't
  6. hasn't been
  7. Did, meet [see] — I didn't💡 last Saturdayという過去の一時点なので現在完了ではなく過去形の疑問文。
  8. Have, known — we have💡 for a long timeで「継続」を表すので現在完了。know-knew-known。
3　例にならって、あとの2つの文を、現在完了の「継続」を表す用法を使って1つの文に書きかえる（例：We moved to this town ten years ago. We still live in this town. → We have lived in this town for ten years.）
  1. I came to this library early this morning. I am still here. → I ___ in this library since early this morning.
  2. They began to practice judo a year ago. They still practice judo. → They ___ judo for a year.
  3. My mother became busy last month. She is still busy. → My mother ___ busy since last month.
  1. have been💡 I have been in this library since early this morning.
  2. have practiced💡 They have practiced judo for a year.
  3. has been💡 My mother has been busy since last month。becomeの状態が今も続いていることをhas beenで表す。
4　＿に、forかsinceのどちらか適する方を書く
  1. I have used the computer ___ ten years.
  2. They have been my good friends ___ then.
  3. He hasn't played baseball ___ a long time.
  4. It has been cold ___ Monday.
  1. for💡 ten years（期間の長さ）なのでfor。
  2. since💡 then（起点）なのでsince。
  3. for
  4. since💡 Monday（起点となる曜日）なのでsince。
5　日本文に合う英文になるように、（　）内の語句を並べかえる
  1. 父はきのうから大阪にいます。（been / has / since / my father / in Osaka / yesterday）.
  2. 2か月間ずっと雨が降っていません。（for / it / not / two months / has / rained）.
  3. あなたは半年間ずっとその英語の番組を見ているのですか。（the English program / half a year / for / have / watched / you）?
  4. 彼はいつからその店に勤めているのですか。（for / has / how / he / long / the shop / worked）?
  5. お久しぶりです。（a long time / for / have / I / not / you / seen）.
  1. My father has been in Osaka since yesterday.
  2. It has not rained for two months.
  3. Have you watched the English program for half a year?
  4. How long has he worked for the shop?
  5. I have not seen you for a long time.💡 直訳「長い間あなたに会っていません」＝「お久しぶりです」の決まり文句。
6　次の日本文を英語に直す
  1. わたしは15年間ずっと日本に住んでいます。
  2. わたしの兄はそのときからずっと新しい自転車をほしがっています。
  3. わたしには先月からずっと彼女から便りがありません。
  4. あなたのお姉さんは昨夜からずっと忙しいのですか。— いいえ、火曜日からずっと忙しいのです。
  5. 彼らはどのくらいの間、ここにいるのですか。— 彼らは1時間ずっとここにいます。
  6. あなたのおばあさんはいつから病気なのですか。— ひと月前からです。
  1. I have [I've] lived in Japan for fifteen years.
  2. My brother has wanted a new bike [bicycle] since then [that time].
  3. I have not [haven't] heard from her since last month.
  4. Has your sister been busy since last night? — No, she hasn't [has not]. She has [She's] been busy since Tuesday. または No, she has [she's] been busy since Tuesday.
  5. How long have they been [stayed] here? — They have [They've] been [stayed] here for an hour.
  6. How long has your grandmother been sick? — She has [She's] been sick for a month.
