演習問題（教科書 p.151-153）
1　●whoの用法●　例にならって、あとの2つの文をwhoを使って1つの文に書きかえる（例：I have a friend. She speaks English very well. → I have a friend who speaks English very well.）
  1. At the party I saw a girl. She knows you.
  2. Do you know the boy? He broke that window.
  3. The girls were going to the stadium. They asked me the way.
  1. At the party I saw a girl who knows you.💡 主語 She を who に。
  2. Do you know the boy who broke that window?
  3. The girls who asked me the way were going to the stadium.💡 主語を修飾するときは文の中に入れる。
2　●whichの用法（主格）●　例にならって、あとの2つの文をwhichを使って1つの文に書きかえる
  1. This is a book. It tells us how to use the Internet.
  2. I like songs. They make me happy.
  1. This is a book which tells us how to use the Internet.
  2. I like songs which make me happy.💡 songs が複数なのでmake。
3　●whichの用法（目的格）●　例にならって、あとの2つの文をwhichを使って1つの文に書きかえる（例：I like the guitar. My brother gave it to me. → I like the guitar which my brother gave to me.）
  1. These are books. I read them last month.
  2. The dog is a star of my family now. My sister brought it home.
  1. These are books which I read last month.💡 them が消える。
  2. The dog which my sister brought home is a star of my family now.
4　●thatの用法●　例にならって、あとの2つの文をthatを使って1つの文に書きかえる
  1. Chris is the boy. He is sitting next to Sarah.
  2. These are birds. They cannot fly high.
  3. The hat was too big. My aunt bought it for me.
  4. The woman drew me a map. I asked her the way.
  1. Chris is the boy that is sitting next to Sarah.
  2. These are birds that cannot fly high.
  3. The hat that my aunt bought for me was too big.
  4. The woman that I asked the way drew me a map.
5　●関係代名詞の使い分け●　次の＿に、who, which, thatのうち適する語を書く（whoでもthatでもよい場合はwho、whichでもthatでもよい場合はwhich）。また、できた文を日本語に直す
  1. I'm looking for a bag ___ has a lot of pockets.
  2. I have an aunt ___ likes talking with foreign people.
  3. The country ___ she wants to visit is Korea.
  4. The old woman ___ I helped at the station couldn't walk fast.
  1. which　わたしはポケットがたくさんあるかばんを探しています。💡 物（a bag）＋主格。whichかthat → which。
  2. who　わたしには外国人と話すのが好きなおばがいます。💡 人（an aunt）＋主格。whoかthat → who。
  3. which　彼女が訪れたい国は韓国です。💡 物（The country）＋目的格。
  4. that　わたしが駅で助けた老婦人は速く歩けませんでした。💡 先行詞に「老婦人（the old woman）」がつき、人でも that が自然。（解答は that）
6　●名詞を修飾する節●　次の英文を参考にして、あとの日本文に合う英文になるように＿に適する語を書く
  1. I bought the racket last week. → これが、わたしが先週買ったラケットです。 This is ___ ___ ___ last week.
  2. I borrowed a book from John. → わたしはジョンから借りた本を読んでいます。 I'm reading ___ ___ ___ from John.
  3. We helped the boy in the park. → わたしたちが公園で助けた少年は日本語を話しませんでした。 ___ ___ ___ in the park didn't speak Japanese.
  4. He saw the dog this morning. → 彼がけさ見た犬は大きくて白かった。 ___ ___ ___ this morning was big and white.
  1. the racket I bought💡 関係代名詞なしで《名詞＋主語＋動詞》。
  2. a book I borrowed
  3. The boy we helped
  4. The dog he saw
7　●関係代名詞の省略●　次の下線部が省略できる語であれば○、省略できない語であれば×で答える
  1. Yesterday I got a letter which was written in English.
  2. Have you found the notebook which you lost last week?
  3. I'm using the chair that my father made for me.
  4. Only students that are taking the computer class can use these computers.
  1. ×💡 which は was written の主語の働き＝主格。主格は省略できない。
  2. ○💡 which は lost の目的語＝目的格。省略できる。
  3. ○💡 that は made の目的語＝目的格。省略できる。
  4. ×💡 that は are taking の主語＝主格。省略できない。
8　次の日本文に合う英文になるように＿に適する語を書く
  1. わたしにはオーストラリアに住んでいるおじがいます。 I have an ___ ___ ___ in Australia.
  2. 中国は長い歴史を持つ国です。 China is a ___ ___ ___ a long history.
  3. これらは父が旅行中に撮った写真です。 These are the ___ ___ my father ___ during the trip.
  4. 佐藤先生が、わたしが博物館で会った先生です。 Mr. Sato is the ___ ___ I ___ at the museum.
  5. だれかフランス語を話す人を知りませんか。 Do you know ___ ___ ___ French?
  1. uncle who [that] lives
  2. country which [that] has
  3. pictures [photos] which [that], took
  4. teacher that, saw [met]💡 過去の文。
  5. anyone who [that] speaks💡 anyone（人）＋主格。
9　次の（　）内の日本語を英語に直して、全文を書く
  1. I have（ピアノをじょうずにひく姉）.
  2. These are（わたしたちを幸せにしてくれる本）.
  3. Please tell me about（あなたがパーティーで会った人たち）.
  4. （母がわたしに買ってくれたコート）is light and warm.
  1. I have a sister who [that] plays the piano well.
  2. These are (the) books which [that] make us happy.
  3. Please tell me about (the) people (that) you saw [met] at the party.💡 目的格なので that は省略できる。
  4. The coat (which [that]) my mother bought (for) me is light and warm.
10　次の2つの文を、関係代名詞を使って1つの文に書きかえるとき、＿に適する語句を書く
  1. Yuji is the boy. He came to see you. → Yuji is ___.
  2. I want some English books. They are easy to read. → I want ___.
  3. This is the speech. King made it. → This is ___.
  4. The American spoke good Japanese. Bob introduced him to us. → The American ___.
  1. the boy who [that] came to see you💡 Yuji is the boy who came to see you.
  2. some English books which [that] are easy to read
  3. the speech which [that] King made
  4. that Bob introduced to us spoke good Japanese💡 The American that Bob introduced to us spoke good Japanese.
11　次の日本文に合う英文になるように、（　）内の語句を並べかえる
  1. わたしが知っていることはすべてあなたにお話しします。 I'll（all / I / tell / know / you）.
  2. これらはわたしが招待した人々です。（are / that / these / I / the people / invited）
  3. わたしは親切そうな女性に話しかけました。（a woman / I / kind / to / looked / talked / who）
  4. あなたが彼らのためにできる最もよいことは何ですか。（you / is / do / can / what / that / for them / the best thing）?
  1. I'll tell you all I know.💡 all のあとの関係代名詞が省略されている。
  2. These are the people that I invited.
  3. I talked to a woman who looked kind.💡 looked kind「親切そうに見えた」。
  4. What is the best thing that you can do for them?💡 最上級 the best があるので that。
12　次の日本文を、関係代名詞を使って英語に直す
  1. (1) 彼はわたしがときどきバスの中で会う少年です。
  2. (2) ① これらはけさ来た手紙です。② これはわたしのおばがカナダからわたしに送ってくれたはがきです。
  3. (3) ① わたしには読書が好きな姉がいます。② わたしは今、彼女が読み終えた本の中の1冊を読んでいます。
  1. He is a [the] boy that I sometimes see on [in] the bus.
  2. ① These are (the) letters which [that] came [arrived] this morning.
    ② This is a [the] card which [that] my aunt sent (to) me from Canada.
    または This is a [the] card which [that] was sent (to) me from Canada by my aunt.
  3. ① I have a sister who [that] likes reading (books).
    ② I am [I'm] reading one of the books which [that] she (has) finished (reading).
