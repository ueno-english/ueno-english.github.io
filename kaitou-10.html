確認問題（教科書 p.56-57）
1　●能動態と受動態●　日本文に合う英文になるように＿に適する語を書く（それぞれ2通りの表現で）
  1. 日本では日本語が話されます。 ①We ___ Japanese in Japan. ②Japanese is ___ in Japan.
  2. その店ではさまざまな種類のカメラが売られています。 ①They ___ different kinds of cameras at the shop. ②Different kinds of cameras are ___ at the shop.
  3. あなたの工場では女性用のくつが作られているのですか。 ①Do you ___ shoes for women in your factory? ②Are shoes for women ___ in your factory?
  1. ① speak ② spoken
  2. ① sell ② sold
  3. ① make ② made
2　●行為者を表すとき●　（　）内の日本語を英語に直して、全文を書く
  1. This dictionary is used（多くの学生によって）.
  2. Yumi is loved（みんなに）.
  3. This store is always closed（彼によって）at seven.
  4. The classroom was cleaned（彼らによって）.
  5. Some CDs were given to Jim（彼女によって）.
  1. This dictionary is used by many students.
  2. Yumi is loved by everyone.
  3. This store is always closed by him at seven.
  4. The classroom was cleaned by them.
  5. Some CDs were given to Jim by her.💡 to Jimのあとにby herを続ける。
3　●否定文・疑問文●　次の文を、〔　〕内の指示に従って書きかえる
  1. The singer is liked by young people.〔否定文に〕
  2. These TV programs are watched by old people.〔否定文に〕
  3. Many houses were broken down by the typhoon.〔疑問文に書きかえ、yesで答える〕
  4. Edo Castle was built by Tokugawa Ieyasu.〔疑問文に書きかえ、noで答える〕
  5. These pictures were taken in Paris.〔下線部が答えの中心となる疑問文に〕
  6. This violin was made in 1700.〔下線部が答えの中心となる疑問文に〕
  1. The singer is not [isn't] liked by young people.
  2. These TV programs are not [aren't] watched by old people.
  3. Were many houses broken down by the typhoon? — Yes, they were.
  4. Was Edo Castle built by Tokugawa Ieyasu? — No, it was not [wasn't].
  5. Where were these pictures taken?💡 場所をたずねるのでWhereで始める。
  6. When was this violin made?
4　●受動態への書きかえ●　下線部を主語にして受動態の文に書きかえるとき、＿に適する語を書く
  1. He loves his dog. → His dog ___ ___ ___.
  2. My father painted the wall. → The wall ___ ___ ___ my father.
  3. My grandmother sometimes invites me to tea. → I ___ sometimes ___ ___ tea ___ my grandmother.
  4. The flood washed many houses away. → Many houses ___ ___ away ___ the flood.
  5. They speak four languages in Switzerland. → Four languages ___ ___ ___ Switzerland.
  1. is loved, him💡 His dog is loved by him.
  2. was painted by💡 The wall was painted by my father.
  3. am, invited, by💡 I am sometimes invited to tea by my grandmother.
  4. were washed, by💡 Many houses were washed away by the flood.
  5. are spoken in💡 Four languages are spoken in Switzerland.
5　●能動態への書きかえ●　各組の文がほぼ同じ内容になるように＿に適する語を書く
  1. Music is loved by everyone. → Everyone ___ ___.
  2. My cup was broken by my brother. → My ___ ___ my ___.
  3. A bag was made for me by my mother. → My mother ___ ___ a bag.
  1. loves music
  2. brother broke, cup💡 My brother broke my cup.
  3. made me💡 My mother made me a bag.
6　●byを用いない受動態●　「 」内の日本語の意味を表すように＿に適する語をあとから選ぶ（as / for / from / of / to / with）
  1. This desk is made ___ wood.「…でできている」
  2. Plastic is made ___ oil.「…から作られている」
  3. The park is known ___ its rose garden.「…で知られている」
  4. He is known ___ the fastest runner in the world.「…として知られている」
  5. The song is known ___ the world.「…に知られている」
  6. The top of the mountain is covered ___ snow.「…でおおわれている」
  1. of
  2. from
  3. for
  4. as
  5. to
  6. with
7　●「〜される」と訳さない受動態●　下線部に注意して日本語に直す
  1. My brother is interested in Japanese movies.
  2. I was impressed with his speech.
  3. The park was filled with children and their mothers.
  4. This street is crowded with a lot of cars.
  1. わたしの兄［弟］は日本の映画に興味があります。
  2. わたしは彼のスピーチに感動しました。
  3. その公園は子どもたちとその母親たちでいっぱいでした。
  4. この通りはたくさんの車で混雑しています。
練成問題（教科書 p.58-59）
1　各組の文がほぼ同じ内容になるように＿に適する語を書く
  1. Many children watch the TV program. → The TV program ___ ___ many children.
  2. This curry was made by my father. → ___ ___ ___ this curry.
  3. The earthquake broke many houses down. → Many houses ___ ___ down ___ the earthquake.
  4. My grandfather gave me an old watch. → An old watch ___ ___ to me ___ my grandfather.
  5. They sell fresh fruit at the store. → Fresh fruit ___ ___ ___ the store.
  6. Did he invite you to the party? — Yes, he did. → ___ you ___ to the party ___ him? — Yes, ___.
  7. When is your birthday? → When were you ___?
  8. How old is your house? — It is one year old. → When was your house ___? — It ___ one year ago.
  1. is watched by
  2. My father made
  3. were broken, by
  4. was given, by
  5. is sold at
  6. Were, invited, by — I was
  7. born💡「誕生日はいつ」＝「いつ生まれたか」と考える。
  8. built — was built
2　次の文を、下線部を主語にした文に書きかえる
  1. Many students study English.
  2. Keiko didn't open the box soon after dinner.
  3. They were invited to the party by her.
  4. My sister broke my cup yesterday.
  5. These cars are usually washed by my father.
  6. She read these books.
  7. The pictures weren't shown to us by Mike.
  8. Do they sell many kinds of fruits at the store?
  1. English is studied by many students.
  2. The box was not [wasn't] opened by Keiko soon after dinner.
  3. She invited them to the party.💡 受動態→能動態への書きかえ。herが主語になる。
  4. My cup was broken by my sister yesterday.
  5. My father usually washes these cars.
  6. These books were read by her.
  7. Mike did not [didn't] show the pictures to us. または Mike did not [didn't] show us the pictures.
  8. Are many kinds of fruits sold (by them) at the store?
3　日本文に合う英文になるように、（　）内の語句を並べかえる
  1. 京都は多くの外国人が訪れます。（by / is / Kyoto / many foreign people / visited）.
  2. わたしたちはその写真に驚きました。（at / surprised / were / the picture / we）.
  3. バターとチーズは牛乳から作られます。（are / butter / from / milk / made / and cheese）.
  4. 彼はこの近くの小さな村で生まれました。（a small village / in / born / he / near here / was）.
  5. わたしたちはあなたのスピーチに感動しています。（are / with / impressed / we / your speech）.
  6. 彼女のかごはクッキーでいっぱいです。（cookies / filled / is / with / her basket）.
  7. 彼は偉大な科学者として世界に知られています。 He（as / is / to / known / the world / a great scientist）.
  1. Kyoto is visited by many foreign people.
  2. We were surprised at the picture.
  3. Butter and cheese are made from milk.
  4. He was born in a small village near here.
  5. We are impressed with your speech.
  6. Her basket is filled with cookies.
  7. He is known to the world as a great scientist. または He is known as a great scientist to the world.
4　日本文を、与えられた語に続けて英語に直す
  1. この車は毎日、母によって使われます。 This ___
  2. その手紙は先週、彼によって書かれました。 The ___
  3. 野球はこの国では行われていません。 Baseball ___
  4. ①これらの写真はいつ撮影されましたか。②それらは50年前に中国で撮影されました。 ①When ___? ②They ___.
  5. ①あなたはどんなスポーツに興味がありますか。②わたしはサッカーに興味があります。 ①What ___? ②I ___.
  1. This car is used by my mother every day.
  2. The letter was written by him last week.
  3. Baseball is not [isn't] played in this country.
  4. ① When were these pictures [photos] taken? ② They were taken in China fifty years ago.
  5. ① What sport(s) are you interested in? または What kind(s) of sport(s) are you interested in? ② I am interested in soccer.
5　次の絵の内容を説明する英文を、与えられた語句に続けて書く（①Mikeがパソコンを使っている絵 ②1996.4.10と書かれた写真 ③Kenが窓を割ってしまった絵）
  1. The computer ___
  2. The picture ___
  3. The window ___
  1. The computer is used by Mike.
  2. The picture was taken on April 10, 1996.
  3. The window was broken by Ken.
