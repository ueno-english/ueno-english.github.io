文法演習(1) 受動態（教科書 p.61-63）
1　次の各組の英文を、下線部に注意して日本語に直す
  1. ① We use a "furoshiki" when we carry things. ② A "furoshiki" is used when we carry things.
  2. ① My father caught these fish last Sunday. ② These fish were caught by my father last Sunday.
  1. ① わたしたちは物を運ぶときに「ふろしき」を使います。② 「ふろしき」は物を運ぶときに使われます。
  2. ① わたしの父はこの前の日曜日にこれらの魚をつり［捕り］ました。② これらの魚はこの前の日曜日にわたしの父によってつられ［捕られ］ました。
2　次の日本文に合う英文になるように＿に適する語を書く
  1. わたしはそのパーティーに招待されています。 I ___ ___ to the party.
  2. あの工場ではチョコレートが作られています。 Chocolate ___ ___ at that factory.
  3. これらの写真は去年の夏にわたしのおじによってカナダで撮影されました。 These pictures ___ ___ in Canada by my uncle last summer.
  4. 卵はよく朝食のときに食べられます。 Eggs ___ often ___ at breakfast.
  5. その絵は居間の壁に掛けられました。 The picture ___ ___ on the wall of the living room.
  1. am invited
  2. is made
  3. were taken
  4. are, eaten💡 Eggs are often eaten at breakfast. 頻度を表すoftenはbe動詞のあとに置く。
  5. was put
3　次の文を否定文に書きかえる
  1. English is taught at this school.
  2. Tall buildings are seen in my town.
  3. This park was cleaned last Sunday.
  1. English is not [isn't] taught at this school.
  2. Tall buildings are not [aren't] seen in my town.
  3. This park was not [wasn't] cleaned last Sunday.
4　次の文を疑問文に書きかえ、（　）内の語を使って答えの文を書く
  1. Apples are grown in the village.（yes）
  2. The bridge was built last year.（yes）
  3. This song is loved by many children.（no）
  1. Are apples grown in the village? — Yes, they are.
  2. Was the bridge built last year? — Yes, it was.
  3. Is this song loved by many children? — No, it is not [isn't]. または No, it's not.
5　各組がAとBとの対話になるように＿に適する語を書く
  1. A: Is baseball played by nine players? B: Yes, ___.
  2. A: Were many windows broken into pieces by the earthquake? B: Yes, ___.
  3. A: When was the wall painted? B: ___ three years ago.
  4. A: Do they speak English in Canada? B: Yes, ___. French ___ also ___ there.
  1. it is
  2. they were
  3. It was painted
  4. they do — is, spoken💡 French is spoken there also.（フランス語もそこで話されています。）
6　次の文を、下線部を主語にして受動態の文に書きかえる
  1. My mother cooks dinner.
  2. He brought these chairs here.
  3. Some volunteers always help the old people when they eat.
  4. She didn't help me with my homework.
  1. Dinner is cooked by my mother.
  2. These chairs were brought here by him.
  3. The old people are always helped by some volunteers when they eat.
  4. I was not [wasn't] helped with my homework by her. または I was not [wasn't] helped by her with my homework.
7　次の文を、（　）内の語（句）を主語にして能動態の文に書きかえる
  1. The bike is used by my brother.（my brother）
  2. The song was sung by her.（she）
  3. Raw fish isn't eaten in the country.（they）
  4. What language is spoken in your country?（you）
  1. My brother uses the bike.
  2. She sang the song.
  3. They do not [don't] eat raw fish in the country.
  4. What language do you speak in your country?
8　次の英文が「　」内の日本語の意味を表すように＿に適する語を書く
  1. This doll is made ___ wood.「…でできている」
  2. The park is known ___ its beautiful rose garden.「…で知られている」
  3. The ground was covered ___ snow.「…でおおわれていた」
  1. of
  2. for
  3. with
9　日本文に合う英文になるように、（　）内の語句を並べかえる
  1. 紙は木材から作られます。（from / paper / made / is / wood）.
  2. わたしの祖母は中国で生まれました。（born / China / was / in / my grandmother）.
  3. 彼らはその知らせに驚きませんでした。（at / surprised / they / not / the news / were）.
  1. Paper is made from wood.
  2. My grandmother was born in China.
  3. They were not surprised at the news.
10　次の日本文に合う英文になるように＿に適する語を書く
  1. 彼女は食べることに興味がありません。 She ___ ___ ___ eating.
  2. 最初のオリンピックはどこで開かれましたか。— アテネで開かれました。 ___ ___ the first Olympics ___? — They ___ ___ in Athens.
  3. あなたは何に驚いたのですか。— わたしは彼の写真に驚いたのです。 What ___ you ___ at? — I ___ ___ ___ his picture.
  1. isn't interested in
  2. Where were, held — were held
  3. were, surprised — was surprised at
11　各組の文がほぼ同じ内容になるように＿に適する語を書く
  1. (a) I got a watch from my grandfather. (b) A watch ___ ___ to me ___ my grandfather.
  2. (a) They don't grow rice in the country. (b) Rice ___ ___ in the country.
  3. (a) This is a paper bag. (b) This bag is ___ ___ paper.
  4. (a) Japanese culture is interesting to Mike. (b) Mike is ___ ___ Japanese culture.
  1. was given, by💡 A watch was given to me by my grandfather.
  2. isn't grown
  3. made of
  4. interested in
12　日本文に合う英文になるように（　）内の語句を並べかえる（下線部の語は必要に応じて適する形に直す）
  1. この本は多くの子どもたちに読まれています。（this book / read / many children / is / by）.
  2. その洪水で何軒かの家が押し流されました。（by / were / away / wash / the flood / some houses）.
  3. ブラウン先生は親切な先生として知られています。（a / as / is / kind / know / Mr. Brown / teacher）.
  1. This book is read by many children.
  2. Some houses were washed away by the flood.
  3. Mr. Brown is known as a kind teacher.
13　次の日本文を英語に直す
  1. サッカーは11人で行われます。
  2. わたしはそのパーティーに招待されませんでした。
  3. これらのボールはどこで見つかりましたか。— 公園でわたしの弟によって見つけられました。
  1. Soccer is played by eleven players [people].
  2. I was not [wasn't] invited to the party.
  3. Where were these balls found? — They were found in [at] a [the] park by my brother.
探究問題(1) 英作文（受動態）（教科書 p.64-65）
💬 このページは自由英作文です。下の「解答例」はあくまで一例（模範解答）なので、文法的に正しければ表現が違っていても正解です。
1　次の問いに自分の立場で英語で答える
  1. Are apples grown in your town?
  2. What are you interested in?
  3. When is a sensu used?
  1. Yes, they are. または No, they aren't [are not].
  2. I'm [I am] interested in sport(s).
  3. It is [It's] used in summer. または It is [It's] used when we feel hot.
2　次のような場合、英語で何と言うか答える
  1. 自分の家から富士山は見えないと言うとき。
  2. 目の前にあるいすは木でできていると言うとき。
  3. 相手が驚いた理由をたずねるとき。
  4. 東京タワー（Tokyo Tower）がいつ建てられたかたずねるとき。
  5. 相手の国では何語が話されているかたずねるとき。
  6. 相手がどこで生まれたかたずねるとき。
  7. だれがそのパーティーに招待されたのかたずねるとき。
  1. Mt. Fuji isn't [is not] seen from my house.
  2. This chair is made of wood.💡 材料の形が変わらない「木でできている」はmade of。
  3. Why were you surprised?
  4. When was Tokyo Tower built?
  5. What language is spoken in your country?
  6. Where were you born?
  7. Who was invited to the party?💡 「だれが」＝主語をたずねる疑問文なのでwhoのあとはそのまま〈was+過去分詞〉が続く。
3　それぞれのものを説明する英文を書き出しに続けて完成させる（①湯飲み茶わん ②ふろしき ③ひな人形 ④鳥居）
  1. a yunomi — いつ使われるか
  2. a furoshiki — いつ使われるか
  3. hina dolls — いつ見られるか
  4. a torii — どこで見られるか
  1. It is [It's] used when we drink tea.
  2. It is [It's] used when we carry things.
  3. They are [They're] seen in March.
  4. It is [It's] seen in front of a shrine.
4　対話が成り立つように、絵を参考にして文を完成させる
  1. A: What is your treasure? B: It's a soccer ball. It ___ last year.（去年おばあちゃんからもらった）
  2. A: Is Akebono Museum new? B: No. It ① ___ in 1970. It ② ___ every year.（1970年に建てられた／毎年たくさんの外国人が訪れる）
  3. A: Where was ① ___? B: In Australia. A: Who found it? B: ② ___. His name is Jack. He is 15 years old.（オーストラリアの少年、新種の鳥を発見）
  1. It was given to me by my grandmother last year.
  2. ① It was built in 1970. ② It is visited by a lot of foreign people every year.
  3. ① Where was the new bird found? ② A boy did.
5　里沙（Risa）とトム（Tom）の対話を完成させる（自転車運転のルールのポスターについて）
  1. First, while you're riding a bike, you must not（　）.（携帯電話を使用してはいけません）
  2. Second, while you're riding a bike, you must not（　）with your earphones.（音楽を聞いてはいけません）
  3. To reduce the accidents, the same rules（　）the government last year.（同じルールが昨年政府によって作られた）
  1. ～ you must not use a [your] cell phone.
  2. ～ you must not listen to music with your earphones.
  3. ～, the same rules were made by the government last year.💡 the same rulesは複数形、last yearがあるので過去の受動態were made byになる。
