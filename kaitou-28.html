確認問題（教科書 p.143）
1　●関係代名詞のwho●　例にならって、あとの2つの文をwhoを使って1つの文に書きかえる（例：I have a friend. He is called Ken. → I have a friend who is called Ken.）
  1. I know a girl. She is called Meg.
  2. I have an uncle. He lives in New York.
  3. The boys are members of the soccer club. They are running over there.
  1. I know a girl who is called Meg.💡 先行詞が人（a girl）なのでwho。2文目の主語 She が who に変わる。
  2. I have an uncle who lives in New York.💡 who の後ろは動詞。先行詞が3人称単数なのでlivesとなる。
  3. The boys who are running over there are members of the soccer club.💡 先行詞 The boys が複数なので who のあとは are。
2　●関係代名詞のwhich●　例にならって、あとの2つの文をwhichを使って1つの文に書きかえる（例：This is a hotel. It was built before the war. → This is a hotel which was built before the war.）
  1. This is a chair. It was made in the 1970s.
  2. I like cats. They have long hair.
  3. The shop is popular among children. It sells colorful sweets.
  1. This is a chair which was made in the 1970s.💡 先行詞が物（a chair）なのでwhich。
  2. I like cats which have long hair.💡 先行詞が複数（cats）なので動詞はhave。
  3. The shop which sells colorful sweets is popular among children.💡 which sells … が主語 The shop を修飾するので、文の真ん中に入る。
3　●関係代名詞のthat●　次の日本文に合う英文になるように、（　）内の語句を並べかえる
  1. The Thames is a river（London / runs / that / through）.
  2. There was an accident（changed / plan / that / our）.
  3. （asked / that / me / the woman / the way）couldn't speak Japanese.
  4. Look at（stands / that / on / the house / the hill）.
  1. The Thames is a river that runs through London.💡 that は人にも物にも使える。
  2. There was an accident that changed our plan.
  3. The woman that asked me the way couldn't speak Japanese.💡 「わたしに道をたずねた女性」＝ the woman that asked me the way。
  4. Look at the house that stands on the hill.
練成問題（教科書 p.144-145）
1　例にならって、あとの2つの文をwhoかwhichを使って1つの文に書きかえる（例：My uncle has a car. It was made in Germany. → My uncle has a car which was made in Germany.）
  1. There are many people. They want to see you.
  2. Mike took me to a shop. It sold delicious fish and chips.
  3. The student was Taro. He came into the classroom first.
  4. The letter came yesterday. It was written by my grandfather.
  1. There are many people who want to see you.💡 人なのでwho。
  2. Mike took me to a shop which sold delicious fish and chips.💡 物なのでwhich。
  3. The student who came into the classroom first was Taro.💡 主語を修飾するので文の中に入る。
  4. The letter which was written by my grandfather came yesterday.💡 which was written 〜 が主語 The letter を修飾。
2　次の日本文に合う英文になるように＿に適する語を書く
  1. リサは2年前に日本に来たアメリカ人です。 Lisa is an American ___ ___ to Japan two years ago.
  2. これらはけさ早くつられた魚です。 These are fish ___ were ___ early this morning.
  3. わたしは、いっしょにコンサートに行ってくれる友達がほしい。 I want a ___ ___ goes to concerts with me.
  4. わたしは川端康成によって書かれた本を読んでいます。 I'm reading a ___ ___ was ___ by Kawabata Yasunari.
  5. わたしは、これは子どもたちを幸せにするよい物語だと思います。 I think this is a good ___ ___ ___ children happy.
  1. who [that] came💡 人なのでwhoかthat。過去の文なのでcame。
  2. which [that], caught💡 物なのでwhichかthat。catch-caught。
  3. friend who [that]💡 friendのあとにwho。
  4. book which [that], written💡 a book which was written by 〜。
  5. story which [that] makes💡 which の後ろの動詞は先行詞に合わせて makes。
3　次の各組の文がほぼ同じ内容になるように＿に適する語を書く
  1. (1) a I know a girl. She sings very well. b I know a girl ___ ___ very well.
  2. (2) a The bag has a lot of pockets. It was made by my mother. b The bag ___ ___ made by my mother has a lot of pockets.
  3. (3) a My brother read this book. b This is the book ___ ___ ___ by my brother.
  4. (4) a These pictures taken by Jack are beautiful. b These pictures ___ ___ ___ by Jack are beautiful.
  5. (5) a Do you know that girl with long hair? b Do you know that girl ___ ___ long hair?
  6. (6) a Look at the boy playing the guitar. b Look at the boy ___ ___ playing the guitar.
  1. who [that] sings💡 「歌う」＝ who sings。
  2. which [that] was💡 bag は単数でmadeの前にwas。
  3. which [that] was read💡 「兄に読まれた本」＝受動態。
  4. which [that] were taken💡 picturesは複数なのでwere。
  5. who [that] has💡 with long hair ＝ has long hair。
  6. who [that] is💡 現在分詞の後置修飾は「関係代名詞＋be動詞」に書きかえられる。
4　次の日本文に合う英文になるように、（　）内の語句を並べかえる
  1. わたしにはダンスのじょうずな姉がいます。（a sister / dances / have / I / well / who）
  2. 京都は長い歴史を持つ都市です。（a city / a long history / has / is / Kyoto / which）
  3. 由美はめがねをかけている背の高い女の子です。（a / Yumi / wearing / tall / who / is / girl / glasses / is）
  4. 宿題をしない生徒が何人かいました。（do / didn't / some students / there / their homework / who / were）
  5. これは料理のしかたを教えてくれる本です。（a book / how to cook / is / tells / this / which / you）
  6. スピーチをしている男性は興奮しているようです。（excited / making / is / looks / that / a speech / the man）
  7. けさ作られたケーキはすべて売り切れです。（are / all the cakes / made / sold out / were / that / this morning）
  8. この絵を描いた画家は日本に興味を持っていました。（in / who / was / Japan / painted / interested / the artist / this picture）
  1. I have a sister who dances well.
  2. Kyoto is a city which has a long history.
  3. Yumi is a tall girl who is wearing glasses.💡 who is wearing glasses と現在進行形を含む。
  4. There were some students who didn't do their homework.
  5. This is a book which tells you how to cook.
  6. The man that is making a speech looks excited.💡 that is making a speech が the man を修飾。
  7. All the cakes that were made this morning are sold out.💡 sell out「売り切れる」。
  8. The artist who painted this picture was interested in Japan.
5　次の日本文を、whoかwhichを使って英語に直す
  1. わたしにはじょうずにギターをひく兄がいます。
  2. わたしは泳ぐのが好きな犬を1匹飼っています。
  3. あなたを助けた女性はわたしのおばでした。
  4. 放課後、その教室で見つけられたペンはわたしのでした。
  5. マイクは青い目をした少年です。
  6. あなたは太郎と由美の間に座っている少女を知っていますか。
  1. I have a brother who plays the guitar well.
  2. I have a [one] dog which likes swimming [to swim].
  3. The woman who helped you was my aunt.
  4. The pen which was found in the classroom after school was mine.💡 which was found 〜 が主語 The pen を修飾。
  5. Mike is a [the] boy who has blue eyes.💡 「青い目をした」＝「青い目を持っている」。
  6. Do you know the girl who is sitting between Taro and Yumi?💡 between A and B「AとBの間に」。
